## In simple words / purpose

Maximum Goofy Dashboard is the deliberately terrible comparison specimen for Work Pulse. It spends enormous visual energy on animated radar, orbiting avatars, thought theater, fake percentages, prestige rankings, giant aggregate scores, ceremonial buttons, terminal volume, and executive confidence while helping the operator answer almost nothing.

The route turns a design critique into an observable experiment. Participants can experience the attraction of the spectacle, attempt concrete operator tasks, then enable **Roast mode** to reveal the exact failure class attached to every panel.

## Change

The hidden route provides:

- twelve executive KPI cards with invented precision;
- a rotating radar whose dots carry no durable work identity;
- an orbiting swarm whose avatars substitute for item, run, authority, and receipt identity;
- a fabricated thought stream authored entirely as fixture text;
- an aggregate confidence gauge with no defined maximum;
- token astrology and strategic omens;
- a Gantt view with invented percentages and ETA language;
- a leaderboard that omits task class, accepted output, repair burden, review cost, and freshness;
- a heatmap with no scale;
- a terminal waterfall with volume and zero external receipts;
- a decision roulette and command deck whose controls perform no product action;
- a meaninglessness ledger that counts the panels and admits the absence of operational definitions.

## Roast mode

Every panel has one explicit defect label. Roast mode exposes these labels without changing the underlying presentation:

- no source;
- no denominator;
- no definition or unit;
- no causal link;
- no durable identity;
- no consequence boundary;
- no operator action;
- invented precision;
- motion without evidence;
- fabricated thought stream;
- prestige ranking without task evidence;
- aggregate score that hides its components;
- action controls that imply authority they do not possess.

Roast mode is text-first. The critique does not depend on color, hover, or animation.

## Interaction model

- **Roast mode** toggles exact failure labels and `aria-pressed` state.
- **Amplify nonsense** cycles three deterministic fictional presentation presets.
- **Shift executive palette** cycles three flat-color themes.
- **Begin ceremonial scan** cycles authored radar claims.
- **Generate executive decision** cycles authored executive decisions.
- Ceremonial command buttons update one live-region announcement and perform no other effect.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `R` Roast mode, `A` amplify, `T` palette, and `?` focus the first control.

No interaction uses randomness, timers, network input, browser storage, or provider state.

## Evaluation against Work Pulse

Place Maximum Goofy Dashboard beside the eventual Work Pulse prototype. Ask participants to identify:

1. the exact active candidate;
2. the latest trusted receipt;
3. the one human decision;
4. the highest-fan-out blocker;
5. the ambiguous operation requiring reconciliation;
6. the safe next action.

Record completion time, wrong turns, false assumptions, scroll distance, and subjective comfort. The anti-reference should look extremely busy and perform badly. Work Pulse wins when answers are quicker, evidence-backed, and easier to explain after refresh or worker restart.

## Visual and accessibility boundary

The route is maximalist while preserving the repository rules:

- flat colors, borders, shadows, typography, and inline SVG only;
- zero CSS gradients;
- every numerical or animated panel visibly belongs to a fictional anti-reference;
- every control is keyboard reachable with visible focus;
- native scrolling remains available;
- narrow layouts stack without horizontal dependence;
- reduced-motion preferences disable all animation and transitions;
- every animation-dependent joke retains text.

## Fixture and authority boundary

The route uses local HTML, CSS, JavaScript, authored fixture text, and inline SVG. It contains no external asset, library, image, iframe, remote font, API call, credential, private record, analytics, tracker, browser storage, product mutation, or durable state.

The route remains outside the Labs manifest in the first slice so the anti-reference avoids collision with active prototype manifest work. It is reachable directly by URL after merge and automatic dashboard publication.

## Proof
