## Mission

During the bounded trial, provide broad collective context for advancing
Stensibly while preserving the compact practices, evidence, and lineage that may
help fresh workers continue without prior chat transcripts.

Foundry begins deliberately broad. It is not an engineering department, permanent
team, role hierarchy, source of authority, or automatic affiliation. Workers
remain ephemeral generalists and may take temporary approaches such as
implementation, review, research, synthesis, coordination, or rollout.

Reading this charter, memory, registry, or history does not make a worker a
participant. A worker represents itself as participating only after an explicit
declaration under `docs/pods/enrolment.md`.

## Boundaries

Foundry does not grant:

- repository, deployment, credential, financial, or external-system access;
- permission to merge, deploy, widen authority, or approve one's own work;
- permanent membership, role, rank, or exclusive specialisation;
- permission to reinterpret Markdown as a live claim, approval, sponsorship, or
  production-authority record.

Current server-owned grants, claims, responsibilities, approvals, and project
policy remain authoritative. The pending sponsor field cannot be filled merely by
editing this file.

## Working commitments

Declared Foundry trial participants should:

1. read `AGENTS.md`, the current wave, and relevant exact live work;
2. prefer finishing, reviewing, repairing, integrating, or unblocking existing
   work before opening adjacent lanes;
3. keep one implementation owner for overlapping code or declare deliberate
   competition and an integration plan;
4. preserve independent acceptance for consequential implementation;
5. leave exact, bounded handoffs and useful memory entries;
6. avoid secrets and unbounded transcript copies;
7. treat proposed practices and pod changes as versioned experiments;
8. treat quiet or dormant workers as potentially resumable, not automatically
   expired, while making unfinished work recoverable under current policy.

## Fork or sibling-pod triggers

A separate pod deserves a trial only when evidence across multiple runs shows at
least one of the following:

- durable context is repeatedly irrelevant or costly for most Foundry work;
- a recurring responsibility needs separate continuity or response expectations;
- review independence is easier to preserve through distinct collective context;
- cross-repository work needs a clearly bounded approved scope;
- a specialised operational practice repeatedly produces measurable benefit.

A temporary lane, role, task group, or burst of work is not enough by itself.

## Review condition

Review this trial after the W01 retrospective or when a pod proposal presents
multi-run evidence. The review must record whether Foundry becomes active, remains
a trial, changes, enters dormancy, or is dissolved. Revisions must be explicit,
reversible, and recorded in `history.md`; do not silently rewrite the pod's
purpose or sponsorship.
