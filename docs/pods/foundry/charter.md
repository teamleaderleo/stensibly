# Foundry pod charter

**Pod ID:** `pod_foundry`  
**Charter version:** 1  
**Lifecycle status:** active, provisional  
**Sponsor:** human operator  
**Enrolment:** open, run-scoped, non-exclusive

## Mission

Advance Stensibly's highest-value current outcome while preserving the compact
context, practices, evidence, and lineage needed for fresh workers to continue
without prior chat transcripts.

Foundry begins deliberately broad. It is not an engineering department, permanent
team, role hierarchy, or source of authority. Workers remain ephemeral generalists
and may take temporary stances such as implementation, review, research,
synthesis, coordination, or rollout.

## Current scope

- Project: Stensibly
- Repository: `teamleaderleo/stensibly`
- Current attention: derived from `docs/current-wave.md`
- Coordination surface before MCP enrolment: GitHub issues, PRs, exact-head
  reviews, and durable handoffs

## Boundaries

Foundry does not grant:

- repository, deployment, credential, financial, or external-system access;
- permission to merge, deploy, widen authority, or approve its own work;
- permanent membership, role, rank, or exclusive specialisation;
- permission to reinterpret Markdown as a live claim or approval.

Current server-owned grants, claims, responsibilities, and approval records remain
authoritative.

## Working commitments

Foundry workers should:

1. read `AGENTS.md`, the current wave, and relevant exact live work;
2. prefer finishing, reviewing, repairing, integrating, or unblocking existing
   work before opening adjacent lanes;
3. keep one implementation owner for overlapping code;
4. preserve independent acceptance for consequential implementation;
5. leave exact, bounded handoffs and useful memory entries;
6. avoid secrets and unbounded transcript copies;
7. treat proposed practices and pod changes as versioned experiments.

## Fork or sibling-pod triggers

A separate pod deserves a trial only when evidence across multiple runs shows at
least one of the following:

- durable context is repeatedly irrelevant or costly for most Foundry work;
- a recurring responsibility needs separate continuity or response expectations;
- review independence is easier to preserve through distinct collective context;
- cross-repository work needs a clearly bounded approved scope;
- a specialised operational practice repeatedly produces measurable benefit.

A temporary lane, role, or burst of work is not enough by itself.

## Review condition

Review this charter after the W01 retrospective or when a pod proposal presents
multi-run evidence. Revisions must be explicit, reversible, and recorded in
`history.md`; do not silently rewrite the pod's purpose.
